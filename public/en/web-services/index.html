What we do

One point of contact for everything your website needs to run, from design to server.

Web design and development

Company websites, landing pages, Shopify stores and custom web apps: fast, mobile-first and easy to maintain. We work with HTML and CSS, React, Python and Shopify.

Hosting and servers

We publish your website on a hosting plan or your own VPS and make it ready for production: Docker, backups, monitoring, security updates and separate test and production environments.

Domains, DNS and SSL

Domain registration and transfers, DNS setup, subdomains and redirects, and SSL certificates that renew automatically so your site always loads over HTTPS.

Business email

Email accounts on your own domain, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up properly, so your messages reach the inbox and not the spam folder.

Maintenance and support

Updates, backups, content changes and troubleshooting, with one contact person who knows your website.

Automation and AI

Once your website is running, we connect it to the rest of your business: chatbots and AI assistants, WhatsApp notifications and integrations with your tools.
