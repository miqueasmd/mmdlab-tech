2. What data we process

Only what you send us when you write to us by email or WhatsApp: your name, your email or phone number and whatever you tell us in your message. This website has no forms, user accounts, cookies or analytics tools.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quote: your consent when you write to us and the pre-contractual steps you ask for (article 6(1)(a) and (b) of the GDPR).
  • To provide and invoice the service you hire: the performance of the contract (article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting: legal obligation (article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR).

We do not make automated decisions or build profiles with your data, and we will not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a project, for one year at most. Client data, for as long as the relationship lasts and then for the periods set by law (for example, six years for invoices and accounting records).

5. Who we share it with

We do not share your data with third parties, except where the law requires it (for example, with the Spanish Tax Agency).

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing, ask us to restrict it or to give it to you in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to info@mmdlab.tech saying which right you want to exercise. If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

If the way we process data changes, we will update this page and its date.
