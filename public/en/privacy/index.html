2. What data we process

If you write to us, only what you send us by email or WhatsApp: your name, your email or phone number and whatever you tell us in your message. This website has no forms, user accounts, cookies or analytics tools. The data of our WhatsApp service for businesses is explained in section 8.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quote: your consent when you write to us and the pre-contractual steps you ask for (article 6(1)(a) and (b) of the GDPR).
  • To provide and invoice the service you hire: the performance of the contract (article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting: legal obligation (article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR).

We do not make automated decisions or build profiles with your data, and we will not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a project, for one year at most. Client data, for as long as the relationship lasts and then for the periods set by law (for example, six years for invoices and accounting records).

5. Who receives your data

Our hosting, network, backup, email and WhatsApp providers, which use it only to provide those services to us. Also public authorities, when the law requires it (for example, the Spanish Tax Agency). We do not sell your data. Providers outside the European Union are bound by the safeguards the GDPR requires.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing, ask us to restrict it or to give it to you in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to info@mmdlab.tech saying which right you want to exercise. If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Cookies

This website uses no first-party or third-party cookies and no analytics or advertising tools, so there is nothing to accept.

8. Our WhatsApp service for businesses

What it is. We set up and run other businesses' customer service on WhatsApp, with our Meta app «MMDLab» on the WhatsApp Business Platform. For the conversations between those businesses and their customers, each business is responsible for the data; we process it on its behalf and following its instructions.

What data. What arrives through WhatsApp: the messages and files, the number and profile name of the person who writes, and whether each message was delivered and read. From each business, the data of the WhatsApp account it gives us access to: its numbers and its templates.

What for. Only to provide that service to the business. We do not sell it, or use it for advertising or to train artificial intelligence.

Who receives it. The business, Meta, which runs WhatsApp, and our hosting, network, backup and email providers, which use it only to provide those services to us.

How long. While the service lasts. When it ends, we delete it within 30 days, and it leaves our backups within the following 60 days.

Deleting your data

Write to info@mmdlab.tech at any time; you do not need to disconnect anything first. The data we keep is deleted within 30 days, and it leaves our backups within the following 60 days. If the law requires us to keep something, we tell you what and why. What Meta or other businesses keep is up to them.

  • If you wrote to a business that uses our service, tell us which one and the number you wrote from. That business is responsible for your data: we pass your request to it and do what it tells us.
  • If you are one of those businesses, you can also remove our app's access to your WhatsApp at any time, in Meta Business Suite: Business settings, Integrations, Connected apps.

We answer within one month.
